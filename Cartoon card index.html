My Favourite Cartoon:

MIRACULOUS🐞🐾

"Miraculous is literally peak chaotic cinema. It’s a superhero cartoon set in Paris that gives major Spider-Man meets Sailor Moon vibes. Basically, you have these two high schoolers, Marinette and Adrien, who secretly transform into the superhero duo Ladybug and Cat Noir. They are a total dynamic duo and have each other's backs to save the city from a villain who turns ordinary citizens into supervillains the second they fail a vibe check. The catch? They don’t know each other’s secret identities. Marinette is a huge fan of Adrien in real life and considers him a super close friend, but as superheroes, they are just the ultimate crime-fighting besties. It's a messy, iconic mix of high-stakes superhero action, teenage drama, and pure chaotic friendship energy."
